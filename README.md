## Projects

### Pill sken
<sub>**Rakathon 2026 · AI in healthcare** · 2026</sub>

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://tomasmusi.github.io/media/pillsken.webp" alt="Pill sken — the mobile app and the doctor's view" width="88%" />
</p>

Patients — especially oncology patients — often take many medicines from different doctors and can't recall exact names or dosages at the appointment. **Pill sken** uses AI to turn a photo of a medicine box or a handwritten note into a structured medication list the patient hands to their doctor, who gets a sortable table with checks for **interactions**, **relation to oncology treatment** and **duplicate active substances**.

<details>
<summary><b>How it works</b></summary>
<br />

| # | Step | What happens |
| :-: | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | **Scan** | The patient snaps the box or a handwritten note. AI extracts the name, strength, dosage and active substance. |
| 2 | **Review** | The patient reviews and corrects the data, then saves it to a personal medicine library. |
| 3 | **Manual entry** | No box at hand? Type it in — the app checks the entry against a drug database and flags mistakes. |
| 4 | **Library** | Browse, search, sort and filter medicines by name, dosage and more. |
| 5 | **Share with doctor** | The patient picks medicines and generates a QR code with a PIN valid for one minute. The doctor scans it or enters the PIN. |

**Privacy by design:** anonymous account (nickname only), data shared only in person with a short-lived code, and deleted from the servers after the session.

</details>
